# Mobile-Sales-DashBoard-Motorola-
The Motorola Phone Sales Dashboard provides a clear overview of overall sales performance.  It tracks key metrics such as total sales, units sold, revenue, and profit.  The dashboard helps analyze sales by phone model, region, and time period.  Interactive charts make it easy to identify top-performing products and sales trends.  

⭐ STAR Answer
S — Situation:
“I worked on a Motorola sales analysis dashboard to understand the company's sales performance across different months, cities, brands, products, payment methods, and customer ratings. The challenge was that the sales data contained many dimensions, so it was difficult to identify important trends and patterns from raw data alone.”

T — Task:
“My task was to transform the sales data into an interactive Power BI dashboard that could provide management with a quick overview of sales performance and allow them to drill down into different business areas. I needed to track metrics such as total sales, quantity sold, transactions, product performance, and customer ratings.”

A — Action:
“I first cleaned and prepared the data and created the required measures and KPIs in Power BI. Then I designed the dashboard with interactive filters, including Month, Brand, and Payment Method.

I created several visualizations:

KPI cards for Total Sales, Transactions, Total Quantity, and Average Price.

A monthly sales trend to identify changes in sales over time.

A city-wise sales map to understand geographical performance.

Brand-wise sales to compare brands such as Apple, OnePlus, Samsung, Vivo, and Xiaomi.

A Top 3 models by quantity sold visualization.

A payment-method pie chart showing the distribution of UPI, debit card, credit card, and cash transactions.

A customer-rating analysis to understand how many customers gave ratings from 1 to 5.

A day-wise sales chart to identify which days generated higher unit sales.

I also added slicers so users could dynamically filter the dashboard and analyze specific months, brands, or payment methods.”

R — Result:
“The final result was an interactive sales dashboard that converted raw sales data into an easy-to-understand business report. It provided a single view of overall performance while allowing users to investigate trends by time, geography, brand, product, payment method, and customer satisfaction.
